What people ask us

Will you come out for a small repair?

Yes. A few tiles, a bit of flashing, some pointing — small jobs are welcome, and they are usually the ones that stop a bigger bill later.

Do I need a new roof or just a repair?

Usually a repair. We will look first and tell you honestly. If a replacement really is the better value, we will explain why.

Do you do flat roofs?

Yes — repairs and replacement on extensions, garages and dormers, as well as pitched tiled and slate roofs.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Leiston and work across the surrounding area, including Saxmundham, Aldeburgh and the nearby villages. Ask if you are unsure.

Can I send a photo before you come out?

Please do. It helps us bring the right materials and often means we can give you a rough idea straight away.

When can you get to me?

It depends how busy the week is. If it is leaking, ring and tell us — we prioritise water coming in.
